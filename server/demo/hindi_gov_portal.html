🎯 Feature B4 & D4 Proof of Implementation: Hindi & Devanagari PII

Generic Western PII models fail on Devanagari digits (०–९), Hindi honorifics (श्री, श्रीमती), and Hindi address gazetteer terms (मार्ग, नगर, ज़िला). Aavaran's on-device lib/redact.js normalizes Devanagari numerals to ASCII with index-preservation, executes Verhoeff checksum validation, and masks Indic data before server egress.
